# Highlights: DRAFT

Elsevier journals ask for 3-5 bullet points, each at most 85 characters
including spaces, uploaded as a separate file. Check the exact rule in
the chosen journal's Guide for Authors. Character counts are in brackets
(computed, not estimated); recount after any edit.

- Corroboration gates need text support, extractor recall, and independence (73)
- DocRED: 50.2% of facts have two evidence sentences; 3.4% name both entities (75)
- Extractors realise at most 3.1% within documents and 4.1% across documents (74)
- Counting distinct sources raises precision with two extractors (62)
- DySECT counts repeats by design: 87.5% of usable concepts pass only via repeats (79)
